About Larry's Collection

Named after a beloved dog named Lawrence, this collection showcases a passion for Honda Preludes. Our inventory features a diverse range of vehicles—from beautifully restored examples in showroom condition to exciting projects currently undergoing restoration. Each Prelude is maintained with meticulous attention to detail, whether fully restored or actively being brought back to its former glory. Every vehicle in our collection represents automotive excellence, timeless design, and our commitment to preservation.
